Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a horizontal sprite sheet of a magic projectile for a dark fantasy game, 8 square cells of equal size, arranged as a 4-column by 2-row grid, on a pure solid black background (#000000) for additive blending. The reference images are STYLE references only; match their luminous painterly fantasy VFX finish, but this projectile must be clearly different from the arcane orb: a "memory bolt" in cold blue and violet. Cells 1 to 4 are a looping flight animation of a bolt traveling to the RIGHT: a small bright cyan-white core with a short comet tail trailing to the left, wrapped in a thin violet halo, with two or three tiny translucent anonymous humanoid silhouettes (no faces, no identifiable character) and a few small crystal shards swirling and flickering around the core a little differently in each frame so the loop feels alive. The whole bolt is centered in the cell and compact, about 130 px long by 70 px tall in a 256 px cell, readable at small size. Cells 5 to 8 are a one-shot impact: frame 5 a bright flash of the core hitting, frame 6 a ripple ring and a burst of crystal shards, with ghostly silhouettes spreading outward, frame 7 the ring grows and thins and the shards fly out, frame 8 only faint pale-blue mist and a few fading shards, almost empty. Every cell must have at least 16 px of clean empty black margin on all sides and no light on the cell borders. Strong contrast against black, restrained detail. No text, letters, logos, frame, ground, scenery, faces, or visible grid lines between cells. Source layout: 1536x1024 landscape canvas. Eight invisible 384x384 cells in 4 columns and 2 rows, grid from y=128 to y=895, centers x=192,576,960,1344; top row y=320, bottom row y=704. Top row cells are flight frames 1-4 in reading order; bottom row cells are impact frames 5-8. All pixel dimensions quoted earlier refer to the FINAL 256px cells: multiply those dimensions and margins by 1.5 for these 384px source cells. Each cell will later be uniformly reduced to 256x256 and repacked as final 2048x256. Final frame retains a small visible mist/shard; never a completely empty cell.
