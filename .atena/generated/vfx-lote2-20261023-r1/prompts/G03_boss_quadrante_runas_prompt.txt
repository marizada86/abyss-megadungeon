Use case: stylized-concept. Game VFX sprite sheet for a 2D isometric dark-fantasy action roguelike. Dense pixel-art-inspired painted effect, crisp luminous shapes, clean readable silhouette. Match the line quality, brightness and crispness of the attached reference board of existing in-game effects, but do NOT copy its shapes. No floor, no cast shadow, no backdrop scenery, no characters, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no cell borders, no logo, no watermark.

Rendering: the effect is drawn in-game with ADDITIVE blending, so paint it as bright luminous light on a uniform PURE BLACK #000000 background. Black means transparent: no dark outlines and no dark shapes inside the effect, darkness is only the absence of light. No gray, no magenta anywhere.

Layout: landscape 1536x1024 canvas. EIGHT frames in a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows of equal invisible 384x384 cells, the grid centered vertically (128 px of empty background above and below). TOP ROW = four LOOPING frames of the warning ground sigil, pulsing slowly, frame 4 flows back into frame 1. BOTTOM ROW = four DETONATION frames played once: ignition, eruption across the whole circle, peak, fade-out. Same scale across frames, each frame entirely inside its own cell with at least 30 px margin, nothing cropped, clear gaps. Exactly eight frames.

Effect: a boss danger sigil painted FLAT on the ground as a perfect TOP-DOWN circle (no perspective, the game will squash it into the isometric floor), about 320 px in diameter, centered at each cell centre. Concentric rings of angular runes and tick marks around a central star, radially symmetric with NOTHING that marks one quadrant different from another, because the game cuts it into quarter slices. TOP ROW: dim crimson-orange runes with a slow pulse of brightness travelling around the rings. BOTTOM ROW: the runes flare white-yellow, fire erupts across the entire circle, the peak is an intense orange-white disc with flame tongues, then it fades to embers. Hostile palette: crimson, orange and white-yellow, neutral enough for the game to tint it per boss.
