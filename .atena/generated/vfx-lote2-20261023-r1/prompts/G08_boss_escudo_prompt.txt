Use case: stylized-concept. Game VFX sprite sheet for a 2D isometric dark-fantasy action roguelike. Dense pixel-art-inspired painted effect, crisp luminous shapes, clean readable silhouette. Match the line quality, brightness and crispness of the attached reference board of existing in-game effects, but do NOT copy its shapes. No floor, no cast shadow, no backdrop scenery, no characters, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no cell borders, no logo, no watermark.

Rendering: the effect is drawn in-game with ADDITIVE blending, so paint it as bright luminous light on a uniform PURE BLACK #000000 background. Black means transparent: no dark outlines and no dark shapes inside the effect, darkness is only the absence of light. No gray, no magenta anywhere.

Layout: landscape 1536x1024 canvas. EIGHT frames in a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows of equal invisible 384x384 cells, the grid centered vertically (128 px of empty background above and below). TOP ROW = four LOOPING frames of an energy dome, frame 4 flows back into frame 1. BOTTOM ROW = four BREAK frames played once: first cracks, fracture, shards flying, fade-out. Same scale across frames, each frame entirely inside its own cell with at least 30 px margin, nothing cropped, clear gaps. Exactly eight frames.

Effect: a protective energy dome around a large monster, seen from a three-quarter isometric angle, about 230 px wide and 200 px tall, centered at each cell centre, with a flat oval ground ring at its base. TOP ROW: a translucent hexagon-patterned shell of pale icy blue-white light, bright rim, soft highlights sliding across the surface from frame to frame, no solid fill so the monster behind stays visible. BOTTOM ROW: bright crack lines spread across the dome, it fractures into hexagonal shards that fly outward, then fade. Neutral icy palette so the game can tint it blue or silver.
