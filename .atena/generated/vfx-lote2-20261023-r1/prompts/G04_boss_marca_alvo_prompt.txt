Use case: stylized-concept. Game VFX sprite sheet for a 2D isometric dark-fantasy action roguelike. Dense pixel-art-inspired painted effect, crisp luminous shapes, clean readable silhouette. Match the line quality, brightness and crispness of the attached reference board of existing in-game effects, but do NOT copy its shapes. No floor, no cast shadow, no backdrop scenery, no characters, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no cell borders, no logo, no watermark.

Rendering: the effect is drawn in-game with ADDITIVE blending, so paint it as bright luminous light on a uniform PURE BLACK #000000 background. Black means transparent: no dark outlines and no dark shapes inside the effect, darkness is only the absence of light. No gray, no magenta anywhere.

Layout: landscape 1536x1024 canvas. EIGHT frames in a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows of equal invisible 384x384 cells, the grid centered vertically (128 px of empty background above and below). TOP ROW = four LOOPING SEEK frames of a targeting reticle that slowly rotates, frame 4 flows back into frame 1. BOTTOM ROW = four LOCK-AND-STRIKE frames played once: the reticle snaps shut and turns red, the circle floods with light, peak burst, fade-out. Same scale across frames, each frame entirely inside its own cell with at least 30 px margin, nothing cropped, clear gaps. Exactly eight frames.

Effect: a boss targeting mark painted FLAT on the ground as a TOP-DOWN circle (no perspective), about 300 px in diameter, centered at each cell centre. TOP ROW: a thin amber-yellow ring with four inward-pointing arrow ticks and a small central crosshair, rotating a quarter turn across the four frames, with tiny orbiting sparks. BOTTOM ROW: frame 5 the ticks slam inward and the ring turns bright red, frame 6 the whole disc floods with red-white light, frame 7 peak burst with sharp radial streaks, frame 8 fading embers. Hostile palette: amber then red then white, no blues.
